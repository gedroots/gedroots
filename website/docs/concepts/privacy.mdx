---
title: Privacy and network use
description: What stays on your computer, the few cases where GEDRoots goes online, and how living people are protected.
---

Family trees hold personal data about people who never chose to be in them. GEDRoots is built so that your research stays on your computer unless you decide otherwise.

## What stays on your computer

- Your `.ged` file and your media. GEDRoots has no account, no server and no cloud storage.
- Your settings: those of the tree are in the `.ged` file; those of the app (recent files, AI agent setting) are in your system's settings folder.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no automatic update check.

## When GEDRoots goes online

GEDRoots connects to the internet only for these features, each started by something you do:

| Feature | Connects to | Sends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Map | OpenStreetMap tile servers | The map area you're viewing |
| Place suggestions while typing, **Locate** | [Photon](https://photon.komoot.io), an OpenStreetMap search service | The place name you typed |
| **Search On**, web links, **GEDCOM for Geneanet** | The website, in your browser | What the website's address contains, such as the person's name |

Map tiles are kept on disk once downloaded. Switch the map to **Offline** to stop any connection from it; in that mode, GEDRoots shows only the tiles it already has.

## AI agents

When you [connect an AI agent](/workflows/ai-agents), GEDRoots runs a server that only your own computer can reach: it listens on `127.0.0.1`, requires a secret token, and refuses requests from web pages. The setting is off until you turn it on.

What the agent reads may be sent to its provider, depending on the agent you use. GEDRoots limits what it sees:

- **Living people are hidden.** A person with no death, burial or cremation is treated as living, unless their birth, or a date that places it (their own events, a union, a child's birth, a parent's birth), is more than 100 years ago. A living person's details are refused, and their name is replaced by “Living person” wherever it appears.
- **The agent can't write.** It can only propose records. You review, compare and save them yourself.

## Living people in exports

Exports and the `.ged` file itself contain everyone in your tree. Before you share a file or publish on Geneanet, make sure the living people in it agree, or remove them from the copy you share.
