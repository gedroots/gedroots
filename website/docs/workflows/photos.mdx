---
title: Identify people in photos
description: Tag the people in a group photo, crop portraits to a face, and point a link at the useful part of a register page.
---

An old group photo is only useful if you know who is in it. GEDRoots lets you draw a frame around each face and link it to the person. The photo then shows the names, and each person's portrait is cropped to their face.

## Tag a person in a photo

1. Open the photo: click it in a person's **Images**, or find it in the **Media** list.
2. Select **Media › Tag a Person in the Photo**.
3. Draw a frame around the face.
4. Choose the person in the picker.

Repeat for each person. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to stop drawing.

The photo's details list every tagged person under **Used By**. Click a name to open the person; click **Redraw** to adjust their frame, or **Whole Image** to remove it.

What changes for the tagged person:

- their portrait and thumbnails are cropped to their face;
- the group photo appears among their images.

The frame is stored as a standard GEDCOM 7 crop on the link between the person and the photo, so other GEDCOM 7 software can read it.

## Choose a person's portrait

GEDRoots shows as portrait the first photo of the person. To choose another one, right-click an image in the person's **Images** and choose **Set as Portrait**.

Without a photo, GEDRoots uses, in this order, an image filed in a **Portraits** folder or titled “Portrait”, an image that isn't a record scan, or the first image.

## Point at part of a document

The same frame works on any link to an image, not only to people. Use it to point a source or an event at the right entry on a register page that holds several records:

1. Open the media.
2. Under **Used By**, find the record the frame is for and click **Mark Area**.
3. Draw the frame around the useful part.

On a document with several pages, the frame applies to the page you're on. A link whose frame is on another page shows a **page 2** button that takes you there.
